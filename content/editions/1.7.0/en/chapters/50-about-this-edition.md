Prepared on **11 November 2026** for the published **v1.7.0** release (release commit **a2493c8**). It combines the current user guide with the maintainer's November captures and selected older illustrations where no replacement is needed. Source revisions and image provenance are recorded with this edition.

Current text describes 1.7.0. Captions identify earlier-interface and development examples; their counts and metadata belong to the pictured library. A draft, reviewed choice, running operation and confirmed result are different states. Images are illustrations, not additional test evidence or promises of universal provider/hardware compatibility.
