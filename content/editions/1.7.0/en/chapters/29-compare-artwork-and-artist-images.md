::: figure figure-013
1.7.0: current/proposed album covers. The explicit Save control is further down the dialog.
:::

**1.**  For an album cover, choose **Edit**, then its cover or a cover suggestion. For an artist, hover/focus its photo for **Change image**; for a playlist, use **Change cover**. Choose a suggestion, your own image or an allowed image URL.

**2.**  Compare **Now** and **New**. Check edition, artist identity and image quality. JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF are supported up to 15 MB.

**3.**  Choose **Save to iBroadcast** and wait for verification. A displayed multi-disc album is processed disc by disc.

::: small
An artist photo changes that artist's image throughout the library. A playlist cover changes only that playlist's cover. Original local image/audio files stay untouched.
:::

::: small
Album/artist artwork Undo is available only where previous artwork exists. Tracks without an earlier cover keep the new one; no prior artist image means it cannot be removed through this Undo. Playlist cover changes have no artwork Undo. An uncertain upload/assignment should be checked before another attempt.
:::
