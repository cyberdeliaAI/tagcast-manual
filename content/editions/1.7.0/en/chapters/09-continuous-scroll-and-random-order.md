::: figure figure-027
1.7.0: browsing order, continuous loading and playback are separate controls.
:::

Choose **Continuous scroll** in the page-size selector for supported album and artist shelves. More results appear near the bottom; **Show more** is the keyboard alternative. Tagcast renders nearby chunks while retaining the space of distant chunks. The main document scrolls normally.

Search, filters and ordering reset revealed results. Back/Forward restores revealed scope and scroll position after visiting an album. Choosing a numeric page size returns to pagination. Tracks, favourite artists/tracks, Recently played and the bounded Decades sample retain their own pagination.

::: small
**Random order** reshuffles the complete matching album shelf, across pages, without starting music or changing the queue. Click again for a new order. It preserves the search/filter, returns to the beginning and works with continuous scroll. **Play random album** starts an album; **Shuffle album** changes its playback order.
:::

::: callout
Selection is the loaded scope

Select loaded albums does not select every matching album. Newly loaded cards are not automatically selected. Every tag, trash or metadata operation still requires its own review.
:::
