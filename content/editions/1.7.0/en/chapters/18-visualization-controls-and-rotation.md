Random changes after 30 seconds of visible playback and gives every effect a turn before repeating. Hidden or paused playback does not spend the visible-playback interval. Choose a fixed effect to leave the cycle. Lyrics can remain beside a visualization.

::: small
Effects depend on the browser/desktop audio-analysis and graphics support. MilkDrop-style effects use the bundled local renderer; unsupported environments can have limits. Appearance choices do not change stream quality or save music metadata. Respect reduced-motion settings and use Off when a visual effect is not wanted.
:::
