Use the small globe/language control in the header or **Settings > Interface > Language**. Choose English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish or Japanese. **Automatic · follow system** uses a supported system/browser language and otherwise English. A manual choice takes precedence.

Language changes interface text and local date/number formatting, not artist names, album titles, genres, tags, filenames, lyrics or reports. Open dialogs with unsaved drafts retain their current language and values until reopened. Some server/provider messages remain English; the Wiki and this manual use English.

## Make the sidebar your own

Under Interface, show/hide optional shortcuts and move them up/down. Settings and essential navigation remain available. Hidden shortcuts do not disable a feature; Recently played and 1001 Albums have their own switches. Restore default sidebar resets the arrangement and hides the optional upload-folder/date shortcuts.

Long labels wrap; the sidebar scrolls vertically. Pointer hover or keyboard focus reveals scrollbars, with a bottom fade when more items remain. System accessibility settings may keep scrollbars visible.
