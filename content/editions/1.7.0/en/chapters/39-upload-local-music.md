**1.**  Enable **Settings > Data & exports > Music uploads**, Save, then approve the extra upload permission if asked. Open Uploads.

**2.**  In the desktop app, choose files/folders. Browser use accepts paths on the **server computer**. Prepare & review checks access, formats and identical file hashes; it sends no audio.

**3.**  Inspect ready, skipped and unreadable files, then **Start uploads**. Only the reviewed new files are sent. Pause takes effect between files; Stop interrupts where possible.

::: small
Uploaded means acknowledgement/hash confirmation. **Needs checking** is genuinely uncertain: inspect iBroadcast before preparing those files again. Hash matching finds byte-identical files, not the same song with different tags. Selection supports 1,000 discovered files, 1.5 GB each.
:::

::: small
iBroadcast processes the files and reads their embedded tags. Reload after processing to see new albums. Tagcast does not watch folders, retag originals or sync deletions. Successful selections clear; uncertain/incomplete jobs remain for inspection. Selections/results are not restored after closing.
:::
