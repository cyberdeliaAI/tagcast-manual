::: figure figure-019
Local test, 27 October: four fragments of The 8bits of Christmas, with the existing Various Artists entry chosen as destination.
:::

**1.**  Search **Albums** for the fragments. Tick them, or **Select all on this page**, and inspect any notice about selected albums outside the results.

**2.**  Choose **Merge albums**, select the existing **Destination album** to keep and choose **Review merge**.

**3.**  Check all source albums, resulting tracks and warnings, then confirm the merge once. Wait for **All tracks confirmed in the destination album** before opening the result.

::: small
Use 2-20 complete single-disc entries with the same disc number, up to 500 active tracks. This action is not for combining CD 1 and CD 2 or partial multi-disc sets. Combine Multi-Disc Album Sets must be off.
:::

::: small
The destination supplies title, album artists, album year, disc number and album rating. Tracks keep their IDs, titles, performers, credits, track years, genres, artwork and numbers. Merging does not infer Various Artists, renumber or deduplicate. Overlapping numbers require explicit acknowledgement. Source ratings/local favourites are not transferred. There is no automatic Undo.
:::
