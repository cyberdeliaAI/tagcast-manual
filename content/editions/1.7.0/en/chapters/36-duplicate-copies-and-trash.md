Use **Albums > Filter and sort > Duplicate tracks**, open an album and choose **More > Move to trash**. Repeated positive numbers on one disc are flagged even when titles differ. The same title at different positions or on another disc is not automatically a duplicate.

::: figure figure-018
Earlier-interface example: upload-day/format groups help compare copies. Suggestions are selected only when you choose them; still inspect the review. Cropped detail from the original screenshot.
:::

**Select extra copies** uses the stricter matching-title/number rule and keeps the first. **Select suggested copies** can prefer FLAC over MP3 and a newer upload within one format, while leaving ties or incomplete comparisons manual. Different titles, artists or durations deserve careful listening. Nothing is selected merely by opening the dialog.

For whole albums, tick cards and review **Move selected albums to trash**. In paginated mode, selection applies to the page; Continuous scroll selects loaded results, not every matching album. Hidden selections still count. Library-wide duplicate analysis has a separate reviewed report and explicit approval/resume.

::: callout
Trash is not permanent deletion

Tagcast moves reviewed tracks to **iBroadcast's trash**. Restore through iBroadcast itself. Tagcast cannot restore them or offer automatic Undo. Local music files stay untouched. Check uncertain results before another selection.
:::
