::: table {"widths": [0.37, 0.63], "headers": true}
| Control | Storage / effect |
| --- | --- |
| Album or artist heart | Local account-specific Tagcast favourite; separate from native stars. |
| Album/artist stars | Native iBroadcast rating, subject to its account/features. |
| Track heart / thumbs-down | Native track rating; independent of playlist membership. |
:::

::: small
Use Favourites to browse the appropriate tab, search and open or play results. Changing a heart or rating does not remove music or alter playlist order. Check the visible state after an uncertain operation rather than repeatedly clicking. Local favourites belong to the active account and settings/profile storage.
:::
