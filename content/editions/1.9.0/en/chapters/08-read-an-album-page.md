The cover, album title, album artists, year and track totals describe the displayed album or disc set. Genre chips and native tags are distinct; **+ Tag** opens the tag manager. Stars and the heart have separate meanings.

**Play** replaces the queue with the album in disc/track order. The **Shuffle album** icon starts it in mixed order. **Queue** offers append or Play next. **Edit** opens metadata; **More** contains **Add to playlist**, **Compare track list** and other reviewed album actions.

Tagcast groups discs conservatively: matching title, album artists, year and upload-day set, with distinct disc numbers. Different editions, upload dates or repeated disc numbers may remain separate. An ampersand in a title is not itself a grouping barrier. Avoid merging merely because covers look alike.
