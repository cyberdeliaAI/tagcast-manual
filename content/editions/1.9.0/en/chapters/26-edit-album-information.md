**1.**  Open an album and choose **Edit**. In **Edit album**, adjust title, album artist, release year, disc number and shared genre/composer choices as needed.

**2.**  Type a name or select an existing artist, genre or composer suggestion. A genre is one complete label until you explicitly Split it. Check spelling to avoid near-duplicate artist identities.

**3.**  Use online results only after comparing the artist, title and edition. Fill selected fields or Use all where offered. Search/suggestions alone never save.

**4.**  Choose **Review draft**, inspect every changed record and confirm Save. Wait for the read-back result.

::: small
For several albums by **one album artist**, select their cards and enable only the shared fields you intend to change. Ordinary bulk metadata editing is not a general cross-artist edit. Changing album year leaves track years alone unless you choose the track-year option.
:::

For track-list corrections from a reference release, use **More > Compare track list** first. Only the title/artist choices you explicitly take enter Edit tracks; automatic suggestions and native-lyrics reads are deferred during that handoff. The ordinary editor review and save remain required.
