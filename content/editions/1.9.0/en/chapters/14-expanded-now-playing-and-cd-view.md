1.7.0: Cover / CD switches the presentation; playback and the queue remain shared.
:::

**1.**  Choose Fullscreen or In app window under **Settings > Playback > Now playing view**. The choice applies when the expanded view next opens.

**2.**  Open the expanded-view icon from the compact player. Escape or its exit control returns to browsing.

**3.**  Choose **CD** in **Cover / CD**. The sleeve stays upright while the printed disc turns partly outside it. Choose **Cover** to return; the remembered choice is independent of fullscreen mode.

Lyrics works beside either presentation. The disc stops on pause/buffering and resumes from its angle; reduced motion keeps it still. Artwork-derived backgrounds respect Light/Dark appearance. Switching the view does not restart audio.
