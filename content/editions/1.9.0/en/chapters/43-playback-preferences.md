Compact-player format/quality and track-artist labels can be hidden without altering playback. Fullscreen/In app window controls how expanded Now playing opens. Album cover/Spinning CD controls artwork independently. Online lyrics, cover background and the optional live spectrum are presentation choices.

**Streaming quality**, **Record played tracks in iBroadcast** and device/queue preferences need Save changes. Play reporting starts off; when enabled it reports after more than ten seconds of actual listening and enables Recently played. That page reflects iBroadcast's native list, including other devices, rather than a complete Tagcast log.

::: callout
Experimental remote queues

Queue synchronisation is off by default and needs extra authorization. Use Devices to connect this app's queue or control a device. A controller pauses local audio. Compatibility with a particular iBroadcast player still needs a practical check; keep it off for ordinary local listening.
:::

**Devices** is available from the playback panel. First setup offers the relevant Playback choices after connection and can be reopened from Settings > Interface. Its Premium quality picker does not add Premium choices to a Free account.
