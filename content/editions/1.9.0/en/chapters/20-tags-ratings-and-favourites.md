On an album, choose **+ Tag**; for a single track use its tag action. Choose **Add to all tracks**, **Remove from all tracks** or **Keep as is**, then **Review tag changes**. Confirm the exact labels and affected track count.

For several albums, tick their cards and choose **Tag**. The review includes complete selected sets, including selections outside the current page/filter. Fully confirmed multi-album assignments deselect the reviewed albums; cancellation or uncertainty retains selection. New tag creates an empty native label. Renaming/deleting a tag has global scope; deleting its label keeps the music.

::: table {"widths": [0.37, 0.63], "headers": true}
| Choice | Where it is saved |
| --- | --- |
| Track heart / thumbs-down | Native iBroadcast rating: heart 5, dislike 1, clear 0. These are alternatives for the same rating. |
| Album / artist stars | Native iBroadcast ratings; wait for confirmation. |
| Album / album-artist heart | Local, account-specific Tagcast favourites on this computer. Independent of stars. |
:::

::: small
Favourites has separate Tracks, Albums and Album artists tabs. Local hearts survive restarts but do not sync to another computer. Archived tags cannot be added here; manage archiving in iBroadcast.
:::
