An album's **Play** and a chosen track replace the queue. **Shuffle album** and **Shuffle playlist** randomise playback without changing stored order. **Add to queue** appends; **Play next** fills Up next before the regular queue. Adding to an empty queue can start playback.

Click the selected queue position to jump there. Remove one position with its row control; removing another position keeps current audio intact. Repeat cycles Off, Queue and Track. **Clear queue & stop** removes the remembered local queue. **Save as playlist** reviews a native track list rather than saving an elapsed playback position.

::: callout
The local queue is remembered

Since 1.5.5, account-specific queue order, repeated occurrences, Up next, current selection, Repeat and Shuffle are restored in the same browser/desktop profile and local address. Restoration never starts audio and does not restore elapsed time. Missing tracks are omitted; browser storage and the 10,000-entry limit apply. Clearing site data removes this recovery.
:::

::: small
Queue synchronisation has its own opt-in ownership and takes priority while active. Local recovery is not a guarantee that a queue exists on another device.
:::

Use the right panel's **Playlist** tab for the local playback queue and **Details** for the current queued track. **Devices** opens device/queue controls. This Playlist tab is separate from saved native Playlists.
