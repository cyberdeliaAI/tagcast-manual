Open **Theme** in the header for Auto, Light or Dark, then choose Forest, Ocean, Warm, iBroadcast inspired, Apple Music Classic or Amber. Apple Music Classic uses red/pink accents and sidebar icons; Amber uses amber/gold accents. Changing appearance does not restart audio.

**1.**  Choose **Settings > Appearance** or **Theme > Edit themes**. Start from a palette/saved theme, or use Import JSON.

**2.**  Name the draft and edit Light/Dark variants beside the component preview. Variant editing does not switch the app's Light/Dark/Auto preference. Inherited colours follow the base palette.

**3.**  Use **Save theme** to store it, or **Save & use** to apply it. **Export JSON** exports the valid current draft, including both variants, for another profile.

::: small
Saved themes are local. Import creates an unsaved preview rather than replacing an existing theme. Contrast guidance helps, but inspect the real screens too. Native audio/output controls can retain system styling. More ready-to-import palettes and their attribution are available at [tagcast.app/themes.html](https://tagcast.app/themes.html).
:::

**Amber Midnight** is an importable custom theme, not a seventh built-in palette. Import its JSON, inspect the preview and choose Save & use to apply it. It then appears under My themes in that profile.
