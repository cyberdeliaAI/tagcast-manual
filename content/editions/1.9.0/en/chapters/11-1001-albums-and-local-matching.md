::: small
Enable 1001 Albums in Discovery, then start **Analyse my library** explicitly. Filter found, possible or missing entries and use decade/year controls. A manual **Choose album** / **Use this album** saves only a local link. Check identity and edition; the result does not prove every track is present. Background excerpts retain their source and licence links.
:::
