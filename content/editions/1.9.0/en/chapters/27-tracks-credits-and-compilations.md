**Edit tracks** contains a track list and the selected track's fields. Track title, artist, year, number, genres and credits can differ from the album. Individual track changes take precedence over album-wide changes. Review includes metadata from both tabs; lyrics keep a separate save.

Additional credits retain their role and optional phrase. Composers are composer credits, not a second primary performer. Do not split a collaboration name on an ampersand, comma or slash unless it genuinely represents separate stored credits.

## Various Artists

For a compilation, select **Various Artists** below Album artist. It uses iBroadcast's native compilation identity and clears additional **album** artists in the review. Each track's performer and track credits remain unchanged. Check the before/after list across every disc. For a bulk selection, first enable Change Album artist.

::: callout
Years are separate fields

New years must be 1800-2050; blank or 0 removes a year. The Interface preference **Apply the album release year to tracks by default** only preselects a checkbox in new drafts. It never repairs previous differences or changes an existing draft.
:::

A comparison correction draft can supply selected track titles or artist credits. Inspect all preserved roles, phrases and guest credits in the ordinary review; choosing a release reference does not authorise copying its album artist into every track.
