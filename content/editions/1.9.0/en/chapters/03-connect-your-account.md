**1.**  Choose **Connect iBroadcast** near the bottom of the sidebar, or **Demo · Connect** in the header.

**2.**  Choose **Sign in with iBroadcast**. The desktop app opens a dedicated authorization window; browser use opens the official website in your browser.

**3.**  Sign in, or use **Continue as**, and approve the requested permissions. Return to Tagcast and keep it open while approval is detected.

**4.**  Wait for **Loading library** to finish. Your own albums replace the demo and the connection indicator changes to **Live**.

::: figure figure-001
1.7.0: Tagcast connection dialog; sign-in starts only after the explicit button.
:::

If the authorization window cannot open, follow the displayed browser link and code. A successful authorization page still needs Tagcast to retrieve the result and load the library.

Use **Reload library** for later changes made elsewhere. **Download everything again** in the connected dialog requests a fresh copy when the cache appears stale. A temporary connection failure does not by itself mean your authorization is lost.

::: callout
Extra permissions are optional

Uploads and queue synchronisation request additional permission only when you enable them. Authorizing an upload permission never starts an upload. Existing custom client registrations remain supported.
:::
