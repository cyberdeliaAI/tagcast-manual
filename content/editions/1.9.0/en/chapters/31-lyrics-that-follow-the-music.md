::: figure figure-026
1.9.0: online LRCLIB line lyrics in Now playing, with attribution and the notice that they are not stored in iBroadcast.
:::

Open **Now playing** or expanded **Lyrics**. Choose native-only, iBroadcast-first with Unison/LRCLIB fallback, or Unison/LRCLIB-first with native fallback in **Settings > Playback > Now playing lyrics**. Native-only remains the default.

Public lookup runs only while a listening-page/expanded lyrics view is visible and an online mode is enabled. Unison has priority for usable text, with LRCLIB fallback when enabled. Ordinary line timing highlights a line; supported word/segment timing adds a moving underline. Provider coverage and timing are imperfect.

::: small
Manual scrolling stops following; use **Follow** to rejoin. Seeking, pause and compatible Gapless use the same player clock. Online results remain temporary and are not automatically saved. The provider receives identifying music metadata, not audio or iBroadcast credentials. Closing/hiding the view stops new lookups.
:::
