Prepared on **13 November 2026** for **v1.9.0** (release commit **440199a**), using the canonical Wiki snapshot **095b8c1**. This edition builds on the unchanged 1.7.0 reference, with selected updates checked against release notes, Wiki and released source. Source revisions and image hashes accompany the reproducible build.

Current text describes 1.9.0. Captions identify earlier interfaces and illustrative version numbers. The update-dialog example deliberately shows a 1.7.0 to 1.8.0 offer. Counts and metadata belong to the pictured library. A draft, reviewed choice, running operation and confirmed result are different states.
