1.9.0: Album artists with Filters, Name/Rating/Random sorting and Continuous scroll.
:::

Tagcast starts on **Album artists**. Select an artist to see their albums, or open **Albums** for the whole collection. Search there by album title, artist or genre. Use **Tracks** when you know a song title, performer, album or composer.

**Filters** is separate from the fixed sorting controls. Albums offers Artist, Title, Year, Added, Rating and Random; Album artists offers Name, Rating and Random. Click the active ordinary sort to reverse it. Grid/list and page size remain independent. Random changes browsing order without starting playback.

Back and Forward restore your previous browse view, including searches and pages. Album breadcrumbs return to the artist, genre, tag or other origin. On narrow windows, use the navigation menu. Row actions appear on hover or keyboard focus and remain available on touch.

**Navigation** collapses or restores the left sidebar and remembers that choice locally. Opening the right Playlist/Details panel does not collapse it automatically. Mobile navigation keeps its own menu.
