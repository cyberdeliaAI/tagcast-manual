**1.** Open a complete loaded album or disc set and choose **More > Compare track list**. Opening the dialog only uses local metadata. Choose **Search MusicBrainz** explicitly, then select a reference release.

**2.** Check the artist, title, date, country, format and label. Results have 20 editions per page and at most 200 per query. The comparison supports up to 1,000 audio tracks on each side. Another pressing can have a different track order or bonus tracks.

**3.** Inspect positions, titles, credits and durations. A duration difference over two seconds is flagged; unknown or incomplete data remains uncertain. These observations do not prove identical audio or the correct edition.

**4.** Choose **Use title** or **Use artist credits** only for the rows you want. Nothing is preselected. Where alignment is wrong, choose another reference track manually. A release-level artist fallback is not evidence for a track's performers.

**5.** Choose **Open corrections in editor**. Inspect Edit tracks, then **Review draft** and the normal final Save. Only selected changed titles and artist credits transfer; track numbers, years, duration, album metadata and membership do not.

::: callout
Keep credit meaning

Existing credit identities, roles and phrases are preserved during the handoff; guest credits remain separate. Check the complete before/after review. Comparison never writes to iBroadcast, merges albums or identifies audio by listening.
:::

::: small
The initial editor handoff defers automatic suggestions and native-lyrics reads so you can inspect the chosen corrections. Explicitly selecting a track later resumes the editor's normal lazy behaviour. Stop and check the source edition if the reference is incomplete or ambiguous.
:::
