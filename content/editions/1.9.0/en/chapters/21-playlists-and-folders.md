**More > Add to playlist** on an album includes its discs. A track's action adds just that track; **Save as playlist** in Now playing reviews the queue. Choose a new private playlist or an existing destination and confirm the review. Repeated selected track IDs are retained once in their first position for create/append operations.

::: table {"widths": [0.3, 0.7], "headers": true}
| Goal | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Rename or describe | Edit playlist; leave every track checked to save text only. |
| Remove one occurrence | Row cross, then review. The library track remains. |
| Remove several | Untick tracks in Edit playlist, then review the retained list. |
| Reorder | Reorder tracks: drag a handle, use Up/Down or Move to position, then Save order. |
| Organise folders | Create/rename a folder or add/remove references. Removing a reference keeps its playlist. |
:::

::: small
Search works across playlist pages. Reordering preserves repeated and unavailable positions and does not reorder the playing queue. Deleting a playlist removes its definition, not music. System lists are protected. Above 10,000 positions, browsing/playback is page-based and some full-list edits are unavailable.
:::
