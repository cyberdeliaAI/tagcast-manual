## Cover and controls stay readable

The mobile-width album page stacks its cover above the title, artist, metadata and actions. The cover fills the available width. The navigation menu replaces the full desktop sidebar.

Use the breadcrumb or Back to restore your browse context. Dialogs keep their own close/review controls; Back does not close an editor.

On a narrow Tracks table, focus Track search results and use Left/Right to scroll. Touch devices keep row actions available.

::: small
Light/Dark/Auto, palette and language remain independent choices. Native window borders and output controls can differ by platform.
