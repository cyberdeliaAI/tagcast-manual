**1.**  Choose More > Download album, or select album cards and Download. A displayed set includes every disc.

**2.**  Check albums, destination folder and options. The folder is on the computer running Tagcast, even when the interface is in another browser.

**3.**  Choose Review download to inspect counts and example paths. No audio is fetched yet.

**4.**  Choose Start downloads explicitly. Read completed, skipped and fallback notes. Stop keeps completed files and eligible partials; reopening never resumes automatically.
