## Common terms

**Album artist / track artist:** release-level credit versus performer on a song. **Disc set:** a displayed group of original disc records, not necessarily a single native album ID.

**Genre / tag:** genre fields versus a separate native label assignment. **Favourite / rating:** local album/artist hearts are independent; track hearts are native ratings.

**Draft / review / confirmed:** proposed inputs; the explicit before/after decision; the checked result after sending. **Unconfirmed:** the result is uncertain, not proof of failure.

## Useful boundaries

Album merge: 2-20 single-disc entries / 500 tracks. Multi-album tags: 20 displayed albums / 500 tracks per review. Lyrics file import: 100 tracks/files. CSV playlist import: 10,000 records. Upload preparation: 1,000 discovered files. These limits describe Tagcast workflows; they are not claims about iBroadcast account storage.

Track comparison: 20 reference editions per page, 200 per query and 1,000 audio tracks per side. Playlist and Details are tabs; keyboard arrows/Home/End work within supported tabs. Navigation is an independent sidebar toggle.
