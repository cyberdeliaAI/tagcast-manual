Welcome opens automatically after startup for a genuinely new installation/profile. Connect there with the normal sign-in, or try the sample library. After connection, setup continues without another login or library download. Existing installations can reopen it through **Settings > Interface > First setup**. Opening it does not create another sign-in or change your library.

**1.** Start at **Welcome**. On **Playback**, review quality and the optional Gapless switch. Premium supports its quality choices; Free uses 128 kbps. Gapless remains experimental and off by default.

**2.** On **Lyrics**, choose whether to use online display sources and read their consent. Online Now playing text is separate from saved track lyrics.

**3.** On **Workspace**, choose presentation preferences. On **Optional services**, enable only the services you want. Finish at **Ready**.

Use **Save and continue** to apply the current step. **Back** revisits earlier choices; **Skip this step** leaves saved preferences unchanged and discards that step's unsaved choices. **Set up later** pauses the flow. Completed choices stay applied; progress can be resumed, but unsaved step drafts do not survive restart. First setup never starts a metadata save, upload or audio download.
